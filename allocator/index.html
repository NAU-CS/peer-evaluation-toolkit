Fill this in, then paste the result into the survey. Nothing here is submitted or recorded — it runs entirely in your browser.

1. Team size

Your budget is team size × 100. It never changes.

How the numbers work

100 means they did their fair share. That is the right answer for most people, most of the time. Because the total is fixed, every point above 100 you give one person has to come off someone else.

NumberUse it when…
100They did their fair share
105–110Noticeably more — took on work that was not theirs
120+Carried the team. Needs a justification in the survey
90–95Slightly less. Normal week-to-week variation
80 or belowNoticeably less; others absorbed it. Needs a justification
0–50Did essentially nothing on this half of the project

Every rating a person receives is averaged — each teammate's plus their own self-rating — then divided by 100 to get a factor that multiplies their team grade. One number from you is averaged with everyone else's: on a 3-person team, rating someone 80 while the other two rate them 100 gives a mean of 93 and a factor of 0.93, not 0.80. The factor is capped at 0.60 low, 1.10 high.

Who sees what

Your teammates receive your written comments with your name removed, and are never told who gave which number. Your instructor sees everything, including who wrote what.

On a two-person team your partner can work out your rating from their own result. That is arithmetic, not a policy choice.
