How to use this

  1. Open the section for your team size.
  2. Find the row that best describes what actually happened on your team.
  3. Assign those numbers to real people — the columns are just slots, put the largest number on whoever did the most. Include yourself.
  4. Write one line per person in the survey: nau_email = points

Every row already adds up correctly, so if you copy a row exactly you cannot get the arithmetic wrong. You are not limited to these rows — any whole numbers that total your team size × 100 are fine. These are here so you never have to do the arithmetic yourself.

​

The survey also asks you to type the total. If your total is not 200, 300 or 400 the survey will reject it on the spot — that is your safety net.
