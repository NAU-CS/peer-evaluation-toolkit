Peer evaluation for {{ARC_NAME}} Parts 1–3. The result feeds {{ARC_NAME}} Peer Review Aggregate Grade Adjustment.

Report how the effort was actually distributed on this half of the project. Your ratings become a grade adjustment for your teammates, and theirs become one for you.

The rules

You have N × 100 points to distribute, where N is your team size including you: 2 people → 200 points, 3 → 300, 4 → 400. Whole numbers only, and they must total exactly N × 100.

100 means “did their fair share.” Above 100 means more than their share — which forces someone below 100, because the total is fixed. A written justification is required for any rating below 80 or above 120.

Email addresses

Use the full NAU email address: letters, then numbers, then {{EMAIL_DOMAIN}} — for example {{EMAIL_EXAMPLE}}. Not a nickname, not a personal Gmail, and not just the letters. Getting this exactly right is how your rating reaches the right person; a wrong address means your teammate may not be credited.

Anonymity

Your teammates receive your written comments with all names removed, and they are never told who gave which number. On a two-person team your partner can derive your rating from their own result.

You may submit twice; the last submission counts. Check your arithmetic before submitting.
